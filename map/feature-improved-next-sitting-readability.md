Currently, when there are multiple sub-steps and/or multiple lines of `see` and `stop if` fields, the Step can be difficult to read and difficult to turn away and come back to.

I'd like for this interface to be improved to make the separation of different sections more obvious and easier to return to as I move around our system to carry out the steps. We shouldn't go too far though; I still want to include around the same amount of information on each page (and view, not allowing scrolling) as we do now.
