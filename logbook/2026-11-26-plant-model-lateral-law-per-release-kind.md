# Plant model: K shared, lateral law per release kind (BUG-138)

**What and why.** f0 (`PlantModel`) pooled every accepted row into one apex filter and one lateral pair, so oval (carried) rows moved the offset applied to Columns (free_cup) Throws. The 2026-11-24 review measured it: adding 607 oval rows moved the applied offset at the +62.5 mm Site from -3.3 to +4.8 mm. The residual memory already matched release kind exactly (I-LEARN-1); the plant model under it did not. The owner chose option 3 in TKT-93 (2026-11-25): share K and split the lateral filters by release kind.

## Discussion

**Why option 3 over 1 and 2.** Option 1 gave each release kind its own plant model, so the oval would cold-start its own K. But K is the one parameter that has held across Sittings and Sites (P1 probe: 1.070-1.095 per Sitting and per Site, flat over apex). Splitting it would discard about 4400 rows of evidence on the parameter that transfers best. Option 2 added a carried lateral term with K and s shared. s would then still pool, and the oval's own fit differs from Columns in slope as well as offset (b_x +179 mm, s +1.23 per m against Columns' s of about -0.1 per m), so the slope would still leak. Option 2 also inherits BUG-139's flaw: the VALUE of s is corrupted after a long single-Site run, and the oval flies one Site. Option 3 shares only what is physically shared (the apex gain of the Hand stroke) and keeps the whole lateral law per kind. Option 4 (defer to E7) would leave the defect in place under any Memory that holds both kinds, and the 2026-11-21 Memory already holds 981 carried rows (below).

**The per-ball deltas split with b and s.** The per-ball deltas Delta_b live in the same two filters as b_x and b_y (one axis-0 filter over (b_x, s, Delta_x...), one axis-1 filter over (b_y, Delta_y...)). Splitting the whole filter object per kind (`plant_model._Lateral`) therefore splits the deltas too. A per-ball offset learned on Columns does not carry into the oval. That is the conservative reading while `ball_offsets` stays off (P2(c)), and it keeps each kind's lateral covariance whole (b, s and the deltas are correlated within one filter).

**Shape and API.** `forward`, `forward_rows`, `inverse`, `observe` and `offset_x` take `release_kind`, which defaults to `'free_cup'` so the Columns-only callers and the existing test battery read unchanged. The Learner always passes its context's kind, which `observe` already computed. Every other repo caller (`skill_node._make_learner`, `sim/skills_gate.py::_make_learner`, `tests/hardware/skills_plan_bench.py`) only constructs `PlantModel()` and goes through `Learner`, so none needed an edit. `RELEASE_KINDS` now lives in `plant_model.py` and `learner.py` re-exports it. The read-outs (`theta`, `raw`, `sd`, `sd_unforgotten`, `param_names`, `at_prior`) keep the free_cup names (`K, b_x, b_y, s, deltaN_x/y`) at the same indices and append `b_x@carried, b_y@carried, s@carried[, deltaN_x/y@carried]`. `summary()` adds the carried b, s, applied offset and row counts. On a Columns-only Memory, `at prior:` now lists the three carried names, which is true. The BUG-137 machinery is per kind: each kind's lateral pair has its own unforgotten companion covariance, and the far-Site guard reads that kind's covariance. A kind with no rows is therefore at its prior and applies the identity laterally, whatever the other kind learned. A rejected row's reason now names its kind.

**Control-implication walk-through (plan E2).** One Throw: `executor._command_u` builds the `ThrowContext`, then `Learner.command` computes u_m = f0^-1(y_d) with the shared K and the context kind's lateral law. The residual is formed against the same kind's f0 (`forward_rows(..., ctx.release_kind)`), clipped about u_m (30 mm lateral, 20 mm apex), and installed. (i) An empty Memory gives exactly the identity command for both kinds (tested). (ii) The cap is still about the MODEL command (unchanged code path; I-LEARN-1). (iii) Nothing in the 40 Hz tick changes: the Learner runs at command time. Install-time `validate_cycle` and the Teensy `MAX_DEVIATION` guard are unchanged on the path. The apex part of every command is bit-identical to the old code, because K was already pooled and still is. The failure mode to watch is a step in u between consecutive Throws. Within a run, the new behaviour has the same per-row dynamics as before, only on a kind's own rows. The one discrete change is at the first launch on an existing Memory, quantified next.

**What a live Memory's commands do at the first launch** (replay, Verification below). The 2026-11-21 Memory copy holds 4474 free_cup and 981 carried rows (the carried rows all at x = -0.075, rows 1038 onward). K ends at 1.0878 in both models. The free_cup applied axis-0 offset moves -3.3 → -4.7 mm at +62.5 mm and +9.7 → +11.1 mm at -62.5 mm, by at most 2.8 mm at any free_cup Site (+125 mm: -9.7 → -12.5). b_y is unchanged (-5.3 mm, forgotten toward the recent free_cup rows either way). The commanded u that actually flies (the Learner's, which the residual memory corrects) changes at the first launch by at most **0.4 mm** lateral at ±62.5 mm, 2.7 mm at +125 mm and 1.0 mm at -125 mm, and 0 in apex. That is far inside the 30 mm cap and the `MAX_DEVIATION` margin. These free_cup offsets move because the leak is removed, which is the fix. Here the move is about 1.4 mm, much smaller than the review's 8 mm: that study added 607 rows of a different oval law, while this Memory's carried law is milder (offset +22.3 mm at -0.075). The carried kind moves more, which is also the point. At -0.075 its applied offset goes from +11.0 (pooled) to +22.3 mm (its own), b_y from -5.3 to +4.4 mm, and the oval's Learner command changes by -8.4 / -2.0 mm. The oval is not flown on this Memory before E7.

## Fix

`plant_model.py`: `_Lateral` (one fx/fy filter pair per kind), `PlantModel._lat`, `release_kind` on every map/update, read-outs packed as above, `RELEASE_KINDS`. `learner.py`: passes `release_kind` on `observe`, `forward_rows`, `inverse` and `forward`. `INVARIANTS.md` I-LEARN-1: "pools every accepted row" is replaced by "K pools every accepted row; the lateral law is per release kind and never pools across kinds; a kind with no rows applies the identity laterally". The entry is AMENDED 2026-11-26 and lists the new tests. Tests: four BUG-138 tests in `test_plant_model.py` (leakage with the measured oval law, the carried kind learning its own law at one and two Sites, identity per kind) plus release-kind validation, and one Learner-level leakage test in `test_learner.py`. Existing tests were updated where they encoded pooling. `test_learner._trained` and `_opposed` had built a "converged f0" from free_cup rows and then queried a carried context. They now converge on the query's own kind, with rows outside the query's L2 support, and `_opposed` opposes rows by release velocity instead of by kind. The probe pin compares the free_cup slice of `theta()` and is unchanged bit-for-bit. Assertions that every parameter has left its prior now exclude the carried names. The BUG-139 xfail is untouched and still xfails.

## Day Session 2026-11-27: merged with BUG-139 / the two findings

**Merge.** Merged onto main together with BUG-139 (`2026-11-27-plant-model-process-noise-on-b.md`). The one conflict was `PlantModel.__init__`: BUG-138's shape stands (one K filter, one `_Lateral` per release kind), K takes `lam=cfg.forgetting` as BUG-139 left it, and `_Lateral` builds each kind's fx/fy with BUG-139's random walk on b (`q=[q_b, 0, ...]`) instead of the per-parameter lambda. Each kind's filters keep their own unforgotten covariance and far-Site guard. The PSD invariant test now also gives the carried kind its own single-Site stretch and checks every filter.

**Finding 1, the silent default.** `forward`, `forward_rows`, `inverse` and `observe` defaulted `release_kind` to `'free_cup'`, so a caller that forgot the kind would read and write the Columns law with no error. The kind is now required on all four. Omitting it is a `TypeError`, and an unknown kind is still a `ValueError`. Only the read-out `offset_x` keeps the default, because no command or update reads it. No caller outside the tests omitted the kind (the Learner always passed its context's kind). The test call sites now name `'free_cup'`. New test: `test_bug138_release_kind_is_required_on_every_map_and_update` (fails on the pre-fix module).

**Finding 2, a cold carried kind (diagnosed, NOT fixed).** The gate is the lateral inlier rule in `PlantModel.observe`: the row is rejected if |innovation| >= `inlier_lateral_m` (100 mm) on either axis. The innovation is measured against the kind's own forgetting filter value, which for a kind with no rows is its prior (offset 0). The apex rule widens its band while K is uncertain (`inlier_apex_sd_mult` x sd_K). The lateral rule has no such term, and no consecutive-rejection or re-seed logic exists anywhere in `plant_model.py`, `learner.py` or `memory.py`. Rejected rows also never enter the Learner's residual memory. Probe of 2026-11-27 (a warm free_cup kind, then 300 carried rows at x = -0.075 with a consistent axis-0 offset and 20 mm noise, 5 seeds):
- An offset of up to 120 mm is learned: 294-300 of 300 rows are accepted. The first row whose noise brings it under 100 mm is accepted at a gain of about 0.6 on b, which pulls the law inside the gate.
- At 150 mm, 0-128 of 300 rows are accepted, depending on whether a 2.5-sigma row arrives.
- At 179 mm, 0 rows are accepted. The kind stays at the identity and nothing corrects the Throw.

Historically the free_cup kind rejected only its very first row at cold start (row 0 of the live Memory: 117 mm). Every later row is accepted, because the Columns offsets are -2 to +18 mm. The live carried kind accepted 1083 of 1085 rows (its offset is about 22 mm). So the finding does not bind on the live Memory. It binds for a fresh carried law whose offset is past about 130-150 mm, such as the review's +179 mm.

It is held, not fixed, because both small fixes move the MODEL command, and the Learner's 30 / 20 mm cap does not bound the model command:
- (a) A consistent-run re-seed: accept a row after N consecutive lateral rejections whose innovations agree within the 100 mm band, while the kind is at prior.
- (b) A wider carried prior on b (for example 100 mm), which also moves the fallback threshold.

Either one needs an owner decision. Pinned: `test_bug138_a_cold_carried_kind_learns_an_offset_up_to_120_mm` (passes) and `test_bug138_a_cold_carried_kind_learns_the_ovals_measured_179_mm_offset` (`xfail(strict=True)`).
