# The Banking tilt schedule scores the realised curve (BUG-159)

## Summary

At the live Leg limits (600 mm/s, 8000 mm/s², 200 000 mm/s³) a REST that starts from a moving state could plan over the Leg jerk limit: the self-Throw seed 0 closing REST (0.332 s from 131 mm/s) planned 211 669 mm/s³ on Leg 1 at t = 0.075 s, about 98 % of it the Banking roll, and the plan gate refused it LIMIT_JERK. The Banking schedule's widen loop guarded jerk on Knot third differences; `validate_cycle` reads the cubic Hermite the plan actually is. The widen loop now scores the tilt's own Leg jerk the way the gate reads it. New invariant I-PLAN-18. Owner's choice of option: "the schedule scores the same interpolated curve the gate reads".

## Root Cause

`decompose` emits Knot values plus Knot rates from `_knot_derivative` (central differences inside, one-sided at the ends), and the plan between Knots is the C1 cubic Hermite through them. With central-difference rates an interior span's jerk is exactly 3 times the Knot third difference over dt³ (`12(p0 - p1)/h³ + 6(v0 + v1)/h²` with `v = (p+1 - p-1)/2h` collapses to `3·Δ³p/h³`), and the acceleration is not continuous at a Knot: it jumps, and the gate differences acceleration over a quarter-Knot sub-step (`feasibility._CYCLE_SAMPLES_PER_KNOT = 4`), so each jump reads as `jump · 4/dt` on top of the span jerk. The realised acceleration is a sawtooth and the gate reads 3 to 5 times the third difference. The schedule bounded the third difference (rad/s³ cap, and a discrete-Leibniz composite of the same third difference) against half the Leg jerk limit. At the old 5000 mm/s² limit the tilt acceleration cap (5.05 rad/s²) bound first and hid the gap (peak 96 %); at 8000 (cap 8.09 rad/s²) it no longer does. On the captured REST the old schedule sat at 199.1 rad/s³ of third difference against a 202.1 cap (in budget) while the gate read 211 669 mm/s³.

## Discussion

**s1 against s2.** Both score the realised curve: the tilt-attributable pose built as `decompose` builds it (centroid shift `arm·axis_xy` with the exact rotation-vector axis, the tilt itself, Knot rates from `_knot_derivative`, knot 0 zeroed for a rest seed per I-PLAN-17), read through `hermite_jerk_reads` (span jerk and both sides of each interior Knot at the gate's sub-step) and times the gate's 1.05 margin. s1 maps the pose jerk to Leg jerk with a conservative lever (`|shift jerk| + R_plat·|tilt jerk|`), s2 with the true Leg Jacobian at each Knot's untilted pose. Measured at the live limits (`$S/out/bug159/table.txt`, the previous unit's prototype): s1 over-reads the gate's tilt part by about 1.5 to 2.6 times, so it over-smooths (self-Throw REST peaks 28-40 % of the limit, tilt authority cut, e.g. st1 peak roll 1.38° against 1.71° with s2); s2 matches the gate within 1 % on captured plans (0.9995 and 0.9998 here, tilt-only replay of the REST schedule) and spends the budget it is given (REST peaks 41-50 %). Built: s2 only. The single live caller (`unified_cycle._realize`) passes `geom`; every other `tilt_schedule` caller is a test, so there is no s1 path: `geom=None` takes the robot's `StewartGeometry()`, the skill stack's own default (`executor.py`, `magazine_throw.py`, `trajectory_node.py`). A duck-typed plan without `pos` gets the same read on the static model (arm at the top of the Hand's band, no vertical motion, home-xy Jacobian).

**The third-difference rad/s³ cap leaves the score; it is redundant once the realised read is enforced.** Both bound the same quantity (the tilt's Leg jerk) against the same budget (`cap · TILT_ACCEL_LEVER_MM`, half the Leg jerk limit); the rad/s³ number has no physical meaning of its own (no actuator moves in rad/s³; the tilt is made by the Legs). For a constant lever arm, the static model the cap is derived from: every third difference sits inside an interior span (spans 1..n-3), where the realised read is at least `1.05 · 3 · g · |Δ³θ|/dt³`, with `g` the largest Leg's gain per unit tilt jerk. Measured over all tilt directions at three Platform positions and two cup heights (685 and 1024 mm), `g` is 0.359 to 0.397 of the static lever (494.7 mm). So a realised read inside the budget implies `|Δ³θ|/dt³ ≤ cap / (3.15 · 0.359) = cap / 1.13`: the cap holds with at least 13 % to spare and can never be the binding constraint on the static model. Where the cup moves vertically the per-cycle cap tightens by the lever estimate `Λ` (`_tilt_jerk_lever_mm`, documented as an estimate, not a bound, loose 1.15 to 2.4 times and once 6.3 times short); the realised read computes that coupling exactly (the shift's Hermite carries `vz·axis + arm·axis'`), so the estimate adds smoothing, not protection. The cost of dropping it, measured: two fed hop-entry seam-gate peaks rose (65.7 → 74.4 % and 73.7 → 80.1 %, prototype table), both inside the limit, and the test `test_the_widen_loop_exits_on_the_third_difference_too` (third difference held under a cap a quarter of the free schedule's) still passes unchanged with only the realised read scored. The alternative, both bounds with the stricter winning (re-add `score = max(score, _max_tilt_jerk(out, dt) / (jerk_cap · 3))` after the realised read), was built as a temporary toggle and measured on 2026-11-28: its output was bit-identical to the realised read alone on all 77 `tilt_schedule` calls captured from the hop, oval, self-Throw seed 0 and reload cells at the live limits, and on the held hop Throw at 0.85, 0.9 and 0.95 m. It never binds where measured, so it is not shipped.

**The first guess had to change too.** With the score 3 times larger than the quantity the analytic widths estimate, the floor and the first rung missed, the ladder grew the smoother kernel (the one quantised branch) and the achieved jerk fell to about half the budget for a larger aim: the C-CUP-3 saturation test read 0.96, 0.98, then 0.52 of budget at 0.5°, 1°, 2° of aim. `tilt_schedule` now divides the sizing cap by `_HERMITE_SPAN_JERK_PER_THIRD_DIFFERENCE = 3.0`, the identity above; the sweep then reads 0.90 to 0.98 of budget from 0.5° to 10°, saturating as before. Sizing only: the score is the read itself. The test's aim grid gained 0.05°, 0.1°, 0.2° because the schedule now saturates at a smaller aim.

**What the read leaves out**, each small and each still checked by the gate, which reads the whole plan and stays the authority: `J̇` terms, the change of `J` with the tilt (J built once per call at the untilted poses), the z-float, and the Platform tilt-map correction `_realize` applies after the schedule.

**The held hop reversal, and the least-bad pick that caused it (fixed here).** `test_skills_segments.py::test_a_held_hop_throw_is_inside_the_r4_limits` claims the pre-release hold lowers the hop Throw's peaks. With the realised read alone it did not (0.9 m: held 134 806 against hold-off 95 461 mm/s³; main 107 361 against 123 305). Diagnosis, measured 2026-11-28 (`$S/out/bug159/fin/hop4.py`): the tilt's own read peaks at 144-146 k mm/s³ in Knots 12-20, around the release, where the cup rises at 4166 mm/s and the shift's rate carries `vz·axis_xy`; that region is pinned by the Throw tilt and no widening reaches it, so every attempt is over the 100 k budget and the loop fell through to its least-bad pick. That pick compared the WORST read only, an unreachable release peak that barely changes across attempts, so it chose the narrowest blend, whose pre-release Knots 0-12 read 134 k (a 0.1 degree tilt wiggle at Knots 3-6) against 33 k on main. The third-difference cap does not help (both bounds gave identical output). **The fix** (`_least_bad_attempt`): when no attempt is inside the budget, pick the one with the smallest summed excess over the budget across every read of the series (each span and each side of each Knot, plus the acceleration's one read, all in budget units); an exact tie goes to the later, wider attempt. The first design was a banded worst read (reads within 1 % of the minimum tied, the summed excess as tie-break). It was built and measured first and changed nothing: on the held hop the worst reads of the three attempts spread 3 % (scores 2.011, 1.917, 1.974 against excess 9.3, 8.9, 1.9), outside any band that stays a tie-break, so the narrow attempt still won; a 5 % band reproduces the broken result too. The plain summed excess needs no band, has one key, and has the peak inside it (the peak read's own excess is one term). Its cost is that it can pick an attempt whose worst read is a few per cent higher than another's when the whole series is much lower; `validate_cycle` stays the authority on both. Attempts inside the budget are untouched: the normal exit returns before the pick, and on the 77 `tilt_schedule` calls captured from the hop, oval, self-Throw seed 0 and reload cells every call returned a schedule bit-identical to the build before the pick changed; 4 of the 77 (all hop, calls 3, 8, 17, 19) took the fall-through and the pick made the same choice in all four (the widest attempt, scores 1.04-1.07, was already the smallest-score and the smallest-excess attempt). Result on the held hop at 0.85 / 0.9 / 0.95 m: held 113 753 / 107 361 / 101 644 mm/s³, equal to main's held numbers; hold-off is now 113 988 / 95 461 / 83 848 (main 126 785 / 123 305 / 120 068), because the realised score also lowered the hold-off Throw. So the ordering `held <= hold-off` still fails at 0.9 and 0.95 m: not a regression of the held Throw (it is where main had it) but an improvement of the hold-off one. The test's claim is restated as an open question, not re-pinned here.

**The pinned measurements that moved.** Two re-pinned (in-margin): the raw rung-1 chain 20.16 → 20.48° and the production hold-less carry 20.17 → 20.50°, each 1.3 % of the margin to the 45° cone. Two flagged, not re-pinned: the rung-1 production carry with the hold, 34.71 → 35.37° (6.4 % of the 10.3° margin to 45°; also the level cell's seating in `test_pose_planner.py`), and the level rung-2 cell's Leg jerk, 112 052 → 119 513 mm/s³ (`test_pose_planner.py` pins 111 510; 8.5 % of the margin to 200 000). Neither cell is exhausted (realised scores 70-87 % of budget): the tilt now spends budget the old score withheld.

**Cost.** `tilt_schedule` per call, best of 3 on the 77 captured calls, unloaded, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, main → this change: hop 3.15 → 5.34 ms (1.7×), oval 4.10 → 6.62 ms (1.6×), self-Throw seed 0 2.31 → 3.25 ms (1.4×), reload 1.49 → 3.05 ms (2.0×); maxima 5.5-8.9 → 9.7-10.4 ms. The read is about 0.35 ms per attempt; the Jacobian columns are sliced once per call and the four channels go through one `_knot_derivative` and one `hermite_jerk_reads` call (output bit-identical to the first build on all 77 calls). The 1.5-4× rises in the sweep table were measured on a loaded box (plan wall mean 4.3 s against about 0.2 s idle).

**Solver-worker determinism: a test-harness cause that predates this change.** The previous unit's scoped run had `test_solver_worker.py`'s two round-trip tests fail by about 1e-13 (`hand_rev`) to 1e-11 (`margin`) between the solver worker and the test process. The cause is the BLAS thread count, not this change: collecting `tests/ros/test_skills_plan_bench.py` imports `tests/hardware/skills_plan_bench.py`, which runs `os.environ.setdefault('OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS', '1')`. When the variable was unset, the test process keeps the 6 OpenBLAS threads it started with, but a solver worker spawned later inherits 1, and the Hand channel's result depends on the thread count (the same Throw in one process with 1 and 6 threads differs in `hand_rev` by 1.6e-13, on main and on this branch alike; the tilt and the realised score are bit-identical, `$S/out/bug159/fin/det.py`). Reproduced on main: `pytest tests/ros/test_skills_plan_bench.py tests/motion/test_solver_worker.py -k "round_trips_identically or round_trips_equal"` with the variable unset fails both tests on main and on this branch. The gate exports `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, so both processes match there and both tests pass. Not fixed here (a separate harness bug).

**Tradeoff accepted.** Moving-seed RESTs lose roll: the captured REST's peak roll is 0.458° against 0.959° before (the banking prescription is lagged harder, so the ball is seated less exactly during the stop). That is the budget doing its job: the old roll could not be flown at all at these limits.

## Control-cycle walk: the self-Throw seed 0 closing REST

Seed: pose moving at (-2.3, -131.3) mm/s, roll rate -0.034 rad/s, cup at z 685.5 mm (arm -63 mm, below the tilt centre); 14 Knots at 25 ms; banking on; `_seed_is_at_rest` is False, so knot 0 keeps the stencil rate in `decompose` and in the score alike.

- **Before.** Sizing cap 202.1 rad/s³; the loop's score was `max(accel/8.09, Δ³/202.1, composite/100 000)`. The returned schedule rolled to -0.959° at knot 5 with 5.72 rad/s² and 199.1 rad/s³ of third difference: score 0.985, accepted. `decompose` emitted central-difference rates; the Hermite's span jerk was 3 × the third difference and the Knot-2/3 acceleration jump added `jump · 4/dt`: the gate read 211 669 mm/s³ on Leg 1 at t = 0.075 s (Knot 3) and refused LIMIT_JERK.
- **After.** Sizing cap 202.1 / 3 = 67.4 rad/s³ gives a wider smoother and blend from the start. Each attempt builds `axis_xy`, `shift = arm·axis_xy`, rates `vz·axis_xy + arm·axis_xy'` and `θ'` (stencil at knot 0), reads both through `hermite_jerk_reads` at 4 sub-steps, maps through the 14 Jacobians built once for the call, takes the largest Leg × 1.05, and divides by 100 000. The floor-first search and bisection end at a schedule rolling to -0.458° at knot 4, 2.27 rad/s², 95.9 rad/s³ of third difference, realised score 0.992. The emitted Knots are that series with the same stencil rates; the gate reads 98 603 mm/s³ (49.3 % of the limit), against the score's own 99 247 for the tilt part. Accepted; the REST flies.

## Fix

- `cup_realize.py`: `hermite_jerk_reads` (the gate's read of a Hermite channel), `_gate_jerk_read_constants` (sub-step count and margin imported from `feasibility` at call time; `feasibility` imports this module), `_tilt_realised_terms` (arm, vz, Jacobians once per call), `_tilt_realised_leg_jerk_mmps3` (the score). `_accel_bounded_schedule` scores accel and the realised read only (`jerk_terms` parameter removed). `tilt_schedule` takes `seed_at_rest=` and `geom=` and divides the sizing cap by 3. Deleted: `_tilt_jerk_terms`, `_tilt_leg_jerk_mmps3` (the third-difference composite, no reader left). Docstrings: `_max_tilt_jerk` no longer claims to be what the gate refuses on; `TILT_JERK_LIMIT_DEFAULT_RAD_S3`, `_tilt_jerk_lever_mm` and the widen-loop docstring say the cap only sizes the first guess.
- `unified_cycle.py::_realize` passes `seed_at_rest=_seed_is_at_rest(state)` (the same value it gives `decompose`) and `geom`.
- No limit, cap derivation, budget fraction, tolerance, Guard or `validate_cycle` changed.
