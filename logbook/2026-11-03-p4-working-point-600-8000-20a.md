# P4 working point: 600 / 8000, 20 A, box re-swept

**What.** The leg Sitting limits (launch defaults) move from 350 mm/s / 5000 mm/s^2 to 600 / 8000 (jerk stays
200000 mm/s^3), the `set_limits` acceleration ceiling from 5000 to 10000, and
`leg_curr_limit_a` from 15 to 20 A. This is P4 of `plans/active/two-ball-oval.md`
(decisions 53-55, 55 amended: 20 A operating under a 30 A trip, owner 2026-11-03). The
admissible box is re-swept at the new point and gains nine pose one-ball oval cells
(separation 150, dwell 0.40 / 0.35 / 0.30 x Apex 1.00 / 1.05 / 1.10) for the ramp
Sitting's dwell ladder.

**Why now.** The headroom re-run (`tools/probes/p4_headroom_cells.py`, 2026-11-03, Hand
4500, hold 0; plan § P4) planned the one-ball oval only to dwell 0.35 at 350 / 5000
(0.30 refused, `MARGIN` on leg velocity +2..+4 %) and to 0.30 at 600 / 8000. JB-24's
CATCH installs ran at a median 93.8 % of the 350 mm/s limit; 365 mm/s would have cleared
13 of its 14 refusals.

**Current.** The leg ODrives were written hard 30 / soft 20 A by jugglebot-50 (c9e2de8d,
`logbook/2026-11-03-leg-odrive-current-limits.md`). Configure pushes
`leg_curr_limit_a` to `current_soft_max` at every launch, so the drives see 20 A from the
next launch. The flashed can-bridge (FW 38) keeps 15 A as its cold-start fallback
(`LEG_CURR_LIMIT_A`); the regenerated header carries 20 into the next flash. No reflash
is needed for P4.

**Derived values that move.** The banking tilt-acceleration cap is
0.5 x leg_acc / 494.7 mm (`cup_realize.TILT_ACCEL_LIMIT_DEFAULT_RAD_S2`), so it rises from
5.05 to 8.09 rad/s^2. That is above the 5-7 band WP4 validated on its demo cycle and
below the 10 it refused; the P4 sweep and the headroom probe plan at 8.09, so
`test_the_default_tilt_accel_cap_is_derived_from_the_leg_budget`'s sanity bound moves
8.0 -> 8.5. The realize config follows the live
leg acc limit (`test_build_realize_config_follows_the_live_leg_acc_limit` now reads
the shipped value).

## The box

`python tools/admissible_sweep_run.py --out temp/sweeps/p4 --leg-vel 600 --leg-acc 8000
--leg-jerk 200000 --hand-acc 4500` (BLAS one thread, 33 families: the committed 30 plus
`ovalx040/035/030_pose`), one full round plus the owner's spot check (2026-11-03: round b
re-ran `oval035_level`, `ovalx045_level`, `ovalx040_pose`, all byte-identical). Result:
113 cells, gate_hash 2605fc1bd25e.

- **Pose cells (the default planner):** all 60 Columns, high-low and oval pose cells keep
  or grow their room around (0, 0). Most now reach the full +/-30 mm grid. Every turned
  Columns band is admissible at both angles. Before, 45 deg admitted only 0.925, and
  90 deg admitted 0.875 and 0.925.
- **New one-ball oval cells:** dwell 0.40 and 0.35 have the full +/-30 mm at all three
  Apexes. Dwell 0.30 is admissible, but the +x side is narrow: 1 / 10 / 25 mm at
  Apex 1.00 / 1.05 / 1.10.
- **Shrank:** 4 level self_toss cells, the low-Apex bound of the pose self_toss
  0.45-0.55 cell, and the level Columns 0.95 cell's Apex band (it moved from below the
  target to above it). The refusals are `MARGIN` (509 in the level self_toss log) and
  `LIMIT_JERK` (19).

## Discussion

- **Why higher limits shrink some cells.** The realize config scales with the leg acc
  limit, so the planner commands harder moves against the same 200000 jerk ceiling. The
  tilt-acceleration cap also rose 5.05 -> 8.09 rad/s^2. Neither planner nor family is flown
  on the ramp Sitting, so this was accepted rather than chased; the
  P4 test leaves those cells unpinned on purpose.
- **20 A, not 30 A.** The plan's 15 -> 30 was amended by the owner to 20 A operating
  under a 30 A trip. This is a 10 A margin to the fault, like the Hand's 70 / 80.
- **600 mm/s exceeds the clamp-engaged catch-up (~529 mm/s, YAML `trajectory_op` note).**
  This was accepted: the catch-up figure bounds recovery while the lead clamp is engaged,
  and the MAX_DEVIATION guard stays the authority if a leg falls behind.
- **Sweep rule.** One full round plus a byte-for-byte spot check of three families,
  replacing two full rounds (owner, 2026-11-03).
